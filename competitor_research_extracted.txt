Worklist should differentiate by connecting the daily site record to workforce, material movements and real project

1. Executive summary
Worklist already covers much of the core contractor workflow: sites, workers, attendance, payroll and payslips, 
advances, products and stock, site services and payments, documents, analytics, calendar and notifications. The 
opportunity is not to add a collection of unrelated modules. It is to make existing records flow into a reliable daily site 
workflow and a trustworthy view of project cost.
The strongest local references are Sitora and GharNaksa. HajariBook is a close workforce/payroll reference. Raken 
and Fieldwire provide mature examples of field reporting, tasks, drawings and approvals. busybusy is useful for 
labour/time costing. builDx is notable for an offline-first material/labour/costing workflow.
Recommended product positioning
An offline-capable construction operations platform for contractors and site teams: know what happened today, who 
worked, what materials moved, what work progressed, what it cost and who approved it.

4. Recommended Worklist product opportunities
4.1 Daily Site Report - highest priority
Create one dated report per site. It should be prefilled from records already entered in Worklist, so supervisors do not 
have to enter attendance and material facts twice.
 Site, date, supervisor, report status and submission/approval timestamps.
 Work completed, work in progress, blocked work and delays.
 Attendance/headcount and labour hours linked to the existing attendance record.
 Material receipts, issues, consumption, returns and wastage linked to inventory movements.
 Services/equipment notes, progress photographs, attachments and timestamps.
 Optional weather, safety/quality observations and general notes.
 Professional PDF export and share action, using the existing document/reporting stack.
Suggested workflow: open site -> Today’s Work -> review prefilled attendance/materials -> add work 
notes/photos/exceptions -> save draft -> submit -> approve -> share PDF. Allow drafts offline and sync them 
idempotently.
4.2 Integrated Site Costing
Create a financial overview that brings together labour cost, material cost, service cost and other approved expenses. 
Show budget, actual cost, committed cost (only where purchase orders exist), paid amount and remaining budget as 
distinct figures.
Accounting guardrail
Do not count the same expense twice. A stock purchase, site transfer, material issue and material consumption are 
different events. A transfer is not a new company-wide cost. A wage/supplier payment should not re-add a cost 
already counted as accrued.

4.3 Material Movement Ledger
Where Worklist currently stores balances or adjustments without full traceability, introduce a ledger for receipt, issue to 
site, consumption, return, inter-site transfer and wastage/damage. Each movement should retain quantity/unit, source, 
destination, date, user, reason and optional bill/challan/photo. Corrections should be traceable rather than silently 
overwriting history.
4.4 Tasks and Milestones
Add a lightweight work breakdown by site stage and task. Each task should have an owner/team, start and due dates, 
status, progress, priority, blockers, checklist and photos. Start with a simple list/Kanban and timeline; avoid building a 
heavyweight BIM or full scheduling platform in the first phase.
4.5 Workforce, approvals and safety
Audit existing functionality before building. Candidate improvements include attendance locking before payroll, leave 
and shift handling, site-scoped supervisor permissions, optional QR/GPS verification, worker payslip access, 
advance/loan ledgers, audit history, safety checklists and incident records. GPS or biometrics should never be 
mandatory by default; provide a non-location supervisor workflow and clear permission explanations.
4.6 Nepal-specific usability
Evaluate Nepali/English interface text, BS/AD date display, Asia/Kathmandu timezone defaults, NPR formatting and 
carefully configurable VAT/TDS or RA-bill support. Store dates canonically and convert for display. Do not hard-code 
tax rates or legal rules without current verification.

7. Engineering constraints for Worklist
 Preserve Flutter + Material 3 and the existing Provider -> repository -> SQLite architecture; no rewrite or new 
state-management framework.
 Keep SQLite as the offline source of truth; maintain safe migrations and backward compatibility.
 Use existing Firebase RTDB paths and sync conventions; handle stable IDs, idempotent retries, conflict states and 
attachments deliberately.
 Keep Android and Windows parity, including sqflite_common_ffi, local file access, notifications and PDF handling.
 Do not move database or Firebase calls into widgets if repository patterns already exist.
 Add unit/repository/provider/UI tests for local persistence, migrations, offline work, repeated sync, duplicate 
submit, permission denial and app restart.
 Keep worker information private; do not log tokens, credentials or personal data.
 No broad rewrite. Deliver one vertical slice at a time and document every schema/sync change.
